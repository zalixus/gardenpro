## B. NEGATIVE KEYWORDS
Keep your existing 44. Then:
- **ADD as Phrase negatives** (catch whole families at once): ideas · how to · diy · free · pdf · image · hydroponic · aeroponic · farming · job · course
- **REMOVE** (likely blocking real buyers): [vertical garden design] · [home garden design] · [garden plan design]

## C. LOCALITIES
Do NOT make per-locality keywords (e.g. "gardener HSR Layout"). Handle areas via **campaign Location targeting** (Settings → Locations). The "bangalore / near me" keywords cover search intent.

## D. BIDDING ROADMAP (you're on Maximize Clicks, ₹600/day, Max CPC cap ₹50)
Reality: on **Maximize Clicks you cannot set per-keyword bids** — Google sets them. Your only bid lever is the campaign-wide **Max CPC cap** (currently ₹50). (So the agency's "raise bid on certain keywords" advice doesn't apply on this strategy.)

Your ₹50 cap is likely holding you out of the top spots (top positions on competitive terms need >₹50) and is why you don't spend the full ₹600 — but avg CPC ~₹30 means you're not throttled on normal clicks.

Sequence:
1. **NOW:** rebuild with this keyword list + negatives + tight ad groups + Phase 3 landing pages. **Leave the ₹50 cap unchanged.** (Raising it before cleanup just pays more for junk clicks.) Quality Score rises → higher positions for free + lower CPC.
2. **After 1–2 weeks:** re-check "Search lost top IS (rank)" on your converters (gardener service, garden services near me, garden setup bangalore, garden landscaping). If still losing top to rank → raise cap in a controlled step **₹50 → ₹70**. Watch: top IS should rise, clicks may drop / CPC rise. Keep the higher cap ONLY if those clicks convert to leads.
3. **After ~15–30 tracked conversions (~3–4 weeks):** switch strategy to **Maximize Conversions** (optionally Target CPA). Then Google bids for likely enquirers, not cheap clicks; the CPC cap becomes irrelevant. This is the real long-term fix (Maximize Clicks buys volume, not leads — which is why you got informational junk).
